Headphones recommended. The volume starts low.

chords

Hold notes on the keys (or A W S E D F T G… on your keyboard, Z and X for the octave) and the band names the chord and follows it. It keeps playing the last one until you play another.

band

The band is continuo's own engine, its Swift compiled to WebAssembly: the same notes as the Mac app, which sends them to real synths. Here they play on small synths in the page, standing in for the General MIDI sounds each scene asks for. It plays the built-in styles; the app can also learn voicings and phrases from MIDI files of real playing, which this page leaves out. continuo is a work in progress.
